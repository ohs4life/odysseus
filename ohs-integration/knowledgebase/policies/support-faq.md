What is D.O.M. and where is the Date of Expiration on the bottles?
Per FDA requirements, our products host a D.O.M. (Date of Manufacturing) on all bottles at the far end of the label.

The product's Expiration Date is 2 years from the D.O.M.. However, even after 2 years from the D.O.M., the product isn't harmful to take but it may start to decrease in potency.
