Instructional to purchasing and starting on the program

Have an account on Optimalhealthsystems.com
Log in to your account on optimalhealthsystems.com unless you haven't created one yet. In that case, you'll need to create a free account before you can purchase. 
Ensure you are logged in before proceeding with the purchase.

Click here to create a new account
Login here if you have an account already

Do a search for the word “nutrients” in the search bar
Click on the magnifying glass in the top right corner and type in the word “Nutrients” in the search bar.
Select “Nutrients Rx. Lab Work” from the search results. 
Please note, Nutrients Rx can only be purchased on one account. (For example, if a sibling wishes to purchase Nutrients Rx on your account, they must purchase it on their own account.)

Add it to the cart
After reading about Nutrients Rx and you’re ready to make the purchase, add it to the cart.
Further down the page, you'll find step-by-step instructions on how to purchase Nutrients Rx, just in case you need them.

Resume with checkout
After you've finished shopping, please review your cart to ensure you've selected the correct items. Once you're satisfied, click the "Checkout" button to proceed.

Complete your order
Select a preferred shipping method. If you live near our Pima, AZ facility, you can choose “Pickup Instore”, only if you live near the store where you can pick up your orders in person.
Add a shipping address where you’d like orders to be sent to.
Enter your credit or debit card information if you're paying with a card. If you're using a different payment method, select it now. Once you've verified all your shipping and payment information, click "Pay Now" to complete your purchase.

Check your email inbox
After completing your purchase, you'll receive several emails from us. These include:
Order Confirmation: This email will detail your purchase and provide your order number.
Subscription Enrollment Confirmation:This email will confirm your enrollment in the Nutrients Rx subscription.
Nutrients Rx Welcome Email: This email from Nutrients Rx will contain a link to "Get Started" and activate your Nutrients Rx account.
Select and open the email from “Nutrients Rx. Portal”.
**Our emails may be in the spam folder. Please be sure to check that folder if you do not see it listed in your main inbox.**

Click on “Get Started”
After reading the email, click on “Get Started”. 

Fill out your profile information
This page is to register a profile for your Nutrients Rx portal. 
To get started, please complete the following fields on this page.
Your password must be at least 12 characters long.
It must include a combination of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters (e.g., !@#$%^&*).
Click on “Register” once all fields are filled.

Introduction to your portal
After completing the questionnaire and clicking "Continue" on the final page, you will be automatically redirected to your Nutrients Rx portal dashboard. Complete the steps.
Click on “Download Document” to print off a mandatory document called a PSC Hold.
Click on “Find a Lab” to find the nearest lab to get your blood drawn at.

Find a lab near you
Type in your address or zip code to find a lab near you where you can get your blood drawn.
Once you’ve found a suitable locations, click on “Make Appointment to get an appointment scheduled with them.
**If you live near our headquarters located in Pima, AZ please contact us to schedule an appointment to get your blood drawn in our own private lab.**

At the appointment for your blood draw
Before your blood draw: Print the PSC Hold document and bring it with you to your appointment.
Results timeline: Your blood draw results will typically be available on your portal in 2 weeks. You'll receive an email notification when your results are ready.
From within that same email, you may choose to purchase the customized health pak based off your results by clicking on the button titled “Buy Customized Pak”

Viewing your results
View your results:
Open the email you received about your results and click "View Recommendations."
Log in to your Nutrients Rx portal to view your results directly.
You may choose to purchase a recommended custom healthpak tailored to your needs based off your results by clicking on the “Buy Customized Pak” button.

Recommended nutrients tailored to your health needs
Clicking on the "Recommendations" tab will take you to a page where you can view the specific nutrients included in your personalized health pak. These carefully selected nutrients will support your journey to optimal health.
If you scroll down the page, you'll also find recommendations for additional products that may complement your health goals.

Detailed Blood Work Results
Your bloodwork results will be presented in a format similar to the example on the right-hand side. We've color-coded the ranges to indicate optimal, functional, and clinically low levels for easier understanding.
If you're unsure about the meaning of a specific marker or reading on your bloodwork results, click the question mark next to the marker title for more information. You can also watch a short video for additional explanation.

FAQs
If you wish to go back to your portal you could log into your account from Nutrientsrx.com. 
Remember, you would need to verify your identity each time you log into your portal through the 2-factor authentication system by providing a 4 digit code that will be emailed to you.
A customized health pak is not included in the purchase. 
Once your blood work results are analyzed, you'll receive an email with a link to your recommended customized health pack. To add this pack to your cart, simply click the link in the email you received with your results. This will automatically add the customized health pack to your cart, ready for purchase. Please note that your personalized custom pack is created only after you place your order.
