### Full description
Deep dive: why the RPM Protocol exists
The plateau isn't the peptide — it's the missing nutrients
You've been on Retatrutide for two, three, maybe six months. The weight was falling off — adipose tissue, visceral fat around the organs — dropping like crazy. Blood markers improving. Everything working. And then it just stopped.

Most people assume the peptide stopped working. It didn't. Not even close. Retatrutide is a triple agonist — it hits your GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors simultaneously. In Phase II trials published in the New England Journal of Medicine, participants lost up to 24% of their body weight. That signal is still firing. But fat loss is not just a signal — it's a mechanical process.

The four-step fat-loss process
For your body to actually burn fat, four things must happen in sequence:

• The fat must be released from the cell (lipolysis)

• It must be transported to the mitochondria — this is where L-Carnitine is essential

• It must be burned (oxidized) efficiently

• The metabolic waste must be cleared

Each step requires specific micronutrients. When caloric intake crashes — which is exactly what happens on a peptide — nutrient intake crashes with it. The RPM Kit covers every step. The RPM Pak handles the broader nutrient foundation. The L-Carnitine handles the transport step that no other nutrient can replace.

What happens without enough L-Carnitine
Carnitine deficiency is more common than most people realize — especially for those reducing red meat intake — and the signs are easy to miss or misattribute:

• Muscle weakness or decreased muscle tone

• Fatigue and tiredness

• Irritability

• Inability to lose body fat

That last one is particularly relevant on a peptide protocol. If fat loss has stalled and fatigue has crept in, a carnitine shortfall — compounded by reduced food intake — could be a significant factor.
