That means that the healthy foods you eat and any supplements you take actually make it into your cells, where they can be used to rejuvenate your body and increase your energy.

Gardeners and farmers know about the beneficial effects that mineral-rich, peaty soils have on their crops. As it turns out, our bodies respond to that mineral content in much the same way—with healthy vitality!

If only the produce we find in the grocery store was grown in such healthy soil without toxic chemical pesticides so we could access those minerals directly. 

Fortunately, we’ve found a reliable source of fulvic minerals. By adding back the trace minerals depleted by industrial farming, you can boost nutrient absorption and rid your body of toxic contaminants.
