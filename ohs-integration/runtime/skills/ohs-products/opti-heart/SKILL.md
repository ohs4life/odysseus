Vitamin C - It is a key component in the production of collagen, the main protein found in blood vessel connective tissue. “Analysis of the literature regarding groups at high risk for coronary heart disease reveals that men, the elderly, smokers, diabetics, hypertensives and perhaps oral estrogen-containing contraceptive users have lowered plasma vitamin C levels.”

Vitamin E - A fat-soluble antioxidant, vitamin E is shown to protect cell membranes and blood vessel endothelium. Vitamin E has been shown to increase oxidative resistance in vitro and prevent atherosclerotic plaque formation.
