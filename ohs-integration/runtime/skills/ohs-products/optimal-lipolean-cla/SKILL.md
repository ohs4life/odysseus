### Full description
We have thoroughly researched various potential ingredients for weight loss aids and are confident in selecting what we believe to be the most effective one: conjugated linoleic acid (CLA).

This was not a trivial choice. The earliest research can be tempting to get jump on and create a product for the sake of making money.

So many supplement and weight loss companies have released ineffective and dangerous weight loss products based on limited research. The only benefit has been profit margins that prey on society’s desperation to slim down. Products like aminorex, dexfenfluramine, rimonabant, sibutramine, and so on have been stripped from the market due to causing problems like pulmonary hypertension, cardiac valve disorders, and fatalities. Even many herbs have gone mainstream for a while due to hype, not research. Always check the research for two things: consistency and that it was actually done on humans.

We have let all the dieting and weight loss fads burn themselves out and continued studying the research pouring in. After observing and analyzing data, we present our latest formula, LipoLean CLA, a weight loss aid supported by research.

CLA has been the focus of hundreds of studies, which show huge differences between people who took 3 to 4 grams of CLA per day and those who took a placebo. However, there’s a key part of the research that a lot of people overlook, which has led to confusion in the past: the duration people need to take the nutrient for.

The key balance: taking 3-4 grams of CLA per day for 12 solid weeks. The results: reduced body fat mass by 4% and reduced food cravings.

LipoLean CLA stands out for its purity, rigorous testing, and each capsule containing a full gram. With 120 capsules per bottle, there's no need to worry about running out before completing the recommended 12-week regimen.

Studies consistently showed weight loss up to 400% faster. In a double-blind, placebo-controlled human study published in the Journal of International Medical Research, 20 participants were given either CLA or vegetable oil, and were instructed to take the pills for 12 weeks. At the end of the study, the test group lost 20% body fat while the placebo group lost nothing. Please note: they took 3.4 grams of CLA for 12 weeks.

At an obesity conference, another study substantiated these findings. 180 overweight men and women with a BMI greater than 25% were supplemented with 3.4 grams of CLA per day over a full year. The participants recorded a 9% decrease in fat mass and a 2% increase in lean mass.

A study conducted by the University of Barcelona, Spain, measured the effects of CLA intake in 60 healthy men and women. Subjects who took CLA lost a significant amount of body fat, 78% of it coming off the belly region, while the placebo group actually GAINED fat mass.CLA (Conjugated Linoleic Acid) attacks fat behind enemy lines, going straight inside fat cells and breaking the molecules down so your body gets rid of them. CLA is like your cellular landlord kicking fat out. And there are five ways your body has been shown to benefit from CLA.

In studies, CLA has been shown to increase energy expenditure by increasing basal metabolic rate, promoting increased heat and energy production and by increasing the amount of fat that you burn at rest (also known as lipid oxidation).

CLA’s role in this is interesting. Studies show it tends to help you burn fat indirectly by preventing your body from storing it in the first place. This means that cheat days and cheat meals don’t spell the end of reaching your weight loss goals.

Daily CLA use has been shown to help reduce appetite and promote satiety or feelings of fullness. While helping reduce your appetite may help some people lose weight, it’s also important that you don’t go overboard with appetite suppression. Long term appetite suppression can actually lead to metabolic damage and weight gain over time.

Inflammation is at the heart of many serious diseases ranging from cancer to weight gain. It may sound strange because it’s gone under the radar for so long, but recent studies show that inflammation is a growing problem.

CLA has been shown to have anti-inflammatory properties that may help to explain its anti-cancer and anti-cholesterol effects. CLA has also been shown to help reduce enteric (or intestinal) inflammation as well as systemic (whole body) inflammation. To reap these anti-inflammatory benefits, make sure the CLA is sourced from plants.

Daily CLA use has been shown to increase insulin sensitivity (make insulin more effective) and daily use for 12 weeks has been shown to increase proinsulin, c-peptide and adiponectin concentrations. It’s not a replacement for diabetic medication but it can make losing weight easier by allowing your body to let go of unwanted pounds.

Our formula, LipoLean CLA, is a culmination of extensive research, ensuring a reliable and effective natural support for aiding weight loss.
