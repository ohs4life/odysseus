PlasticTest is the first blood test available to the public that allows you to do exactly that. It identifies the number and size of plastic particles circulating in your bloodstream using cutting-edge imaging and sample analysis.

PlasticTest gives you more than just a number—it gives you clarity, accountability, and a roadmap for better health. It’s also the foundation for Plastic Detox protocols like Chemzyme and the Plastic Detox Pak, so you can take action with precision.
