### Full description
Zinc is an essential mineral that is a component of over 300 enzymes needed to repair wounds, maintain fertility in adults, growth in children, and more. Zinc plays a crucial role in cell division, aiding muscle repair and growth by making it possible for the body to heal faster. It is also an effective anti-inflammatory and antioxidant agent.

Essential Zinc provides a chelated, high bioavailable form called zinc bisglycinate chelate, which has been shown to provide nutrition for proper growth, maintenance, and repair of the human body. This can lead to healthy skin, healthy physical development and healing, and reduce inflammation.
