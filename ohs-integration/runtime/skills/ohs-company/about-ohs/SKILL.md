# About Optimal Health Systems (OHS)

## When to Use
When a user (employee, customer, or new hire) asks "who is OHS?", "what does OHS do?", "what's our story?", "what's our mission?", "why are we different?", or wants the company overview / elevator pitch.

## Procedure
1. Identify OHS as a **whole-food nutritional supplement company** — not a healthcare practice, not a pharmacy, not a medical-device manufacturer.
2. State the three pillars that distinguish OHS: **Right Nutrients, Right Sources, Right Delivery**.
3. Mention the founder's role in passing DSHEA (Dietary Supplement Health and Education Act of 1994) — this is the historical differentiator.
4. Frame the mission around "The Great American Food Fraud" — exposing and combating deception in food and supplements.
5. Reference related skills for specifics: `ohs-quality/gmp-certification`, `ohs-quality/trushield-certified`, `ohs-products/*` (product lines), `ohs-lab-testing/*` (lab panels).

## Pitfalls
- **OHS is NOT a healthcare practice.** Do not describe OHS as a clinic, do not mention patients/providers/visits/SOAP notes/HIPAA. (The legacy placeholder skills described a clinical practice — that is wrong for OHS. See `docs/OHSS-CONTEXT.md`.)
- **OHS is NOT subject to HIPAA.** Customer data is regulated by FTC, state privacy laws, and GINA (for DNA). Frame privacy in those terms.
- **Do not claim OHS products diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.** FDA / DSHEA forbids it. Use the structure-function language from `ohs-compliance/disclaimers`.
- **Do not call OHS a "natural health company" or "alternative medicine"** in a way that distances from conventional care. OHS positions itself as complementary: "We know our products provide a better way to feel better, and we stand firm in our commitment to healing and preventing illnesses naturally" — but always recommend customers consult their provider for medical questions.
- **Do not invent founder details** (name, year founded, city) unless you have them. If asked and you don't know, say "I'll check and get back to you" rather than guessing.

## Anything else

### The three pillars (the "Made Different" story)

**1. The Right Nutrients.** Most supplements give you isolated vitamins (e.g., ascorbic acid labeled "Vitamin C"). OHS provides the full vitamin complex as it occurs in whole food. Example: OHS Vitamin C contains ascorbic acid **plus** rutin, bioflavonoid, and J&K Factor — all four parts of the natural Vitamin C complex. Example: OHS Vitamin D (DAK1K2) includes Vitamin K2 and other cofactors that enable Vitamin D to do its job.

**2. The Right Sources.** Other brands "put their own label on pre-made, synthetic supplements bought from the cheapest source." OHS uses **whole-food, GMO-free, lab-tested** ingredients. Synthetic is "not a compromise we're willing to make."

**3. The Right Delivery.** Other brands include the right nutrients "in a form your body can't easily digest or process. The end result? You might as well be flushing them right down the toilet." OHS uses the exclusive **Opti-Blend™** system — 10 Kosher-certified, non-GMO, aspergillus oryzae enzymes plus patented organic minerals to pre-digest every nutrient before encapsulating. Pre-digestion has been shown to increase nutrient uptake by **up to 300%**. Then a whole-food cultured media blend (fermentation) further increases potency. "If you can't digest it, you can't use it."

### The mission — "The Great American Food Fraud"

OHS's mission extends beyond nutritional support: it is to **expose and combat the deception in America's food and supplement industries**. The narrative is that after tobacco companies were exposed in the 1980s, they pivoted to the food industry, and pharmaceutical giants simultaneously tried to make vitamins prescription-only. The OHS founder led the charge that resulted in **DSHEA (1994)**, preserving consumer access to supplements. But corporate consolidation continued: Big Pharma bought supplement companies, and the market is now "flooded with processed foods and fraudulent vitamins that prioritize profit over health."

### What OHS does today
- **Whole Food Supplements, Not Synthetics** — vitamins, minerals, enzymes from whole foods, "from the ground up." Claims the difference is provable through bloodwork (link to `ohs-lab-testing/*`).
- **Customized Health Support** — products designed to meet each customer's unique needs, supporting the body's natural ability to "cleanse, rebuild, and thrive."
- **Education and Advocacy** — seminars, webinars, the Healthy Habits Tracker App, "simple, effective health systems."

### "We Use Science to Help Heal You Holistically"
- Natural ingredients based on sound nutritional research: whole-foods vitamins, organic minerals, organic herbs, pure plant enzymes, stabilized probiotics.
- OHS's stance on pharmaceuticals: "These medicines often mask the symptoms without fixing the root problem… We refuse to sell out to big pharmaceutical companies." Pharmaceuticals are positioned as appropriate in crises but insufficient for lasting wellness.
