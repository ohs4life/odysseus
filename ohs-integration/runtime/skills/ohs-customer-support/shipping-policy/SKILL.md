# OHS Shipping Policy

## When to Use
When the user (employee or customer) asks any variant of:
- "When will my order ship?"
- "How long does shipping take?"
- "Do you ship to [country / P.O. box / APO]?"
- "What shipping methods do you offer?"
- "My package is lost / damaged."
- "Can I pick up locally?"
- "Do you offer shipping insurance?"
- "Can I cancel my order?"

## Procedure

### 1. Order processing time
- **All orders:** up to **48 hours** processing time before shipment (best effort is faster, but reserve 48 hours).
- **Customized orders** (e.g., Custom Health Pak): **up to 72 hours** total processing time (the extra 24 hours is for the custom build).

### 2. Shipping time estimates
- OHS **does not guarantee** shipping times. Estimates come from the carrier (USPS, FedEx, UPS).
- Customer should use the **tracking number** from the order confirmation with the carrier directly for status.

### 3. Expedited shipping methods (the only "expedited" options)
FedEx Overnight, FedEx 2Day, UPS Next Day, UPS 2nd Day, UPS 3 Day Select, UPS Worldwide Expedited. **All other methods are not expedited.**

### 4. Address responsibility
- **Customer is responsible** for the correct shipping address on orders they place or autoship orders they schedule.
- OHS is **not responsible** for shipments to an incorrect address entered by the customer.
- **No refunds or replacements** for incorrect-address shipments unless OHS is at fault.

### 5. P.O. Box rules
- P.O. Box addresses can only be shipped via **USPS**.
- If customer chooses a non-USPS method for a P.O. Box, OHS updates it to USPS and **refunds any cost difference above the standard $7.95 charge**.
- P.O. Box delivery is **not available outside the contiguous 48 states**.

### 6. International shipping
- OHS offers international shipping via FedEx, UPS, USPS.
- **OHS does not cover** taxes, fees, or import/export charges — all are the customer's responsibility.
- Customer is responsible for understanding the tax / import laws of the destination country.

### 7. Cancellation policy
- Cancellation is possible **up until the order is fulfilled** by OHS shipping.
- Fulfilled orders are not eligible for cancellation.

### 8. Local pickup (Graham County, AZ only)
- Available to **Graham County, Arizona** residents at the Pima, AZ store.
- Standard pickup: **3 days** from notification. After 3 days, the order is canceled and refunded.
- **Custom Paks**: held up to **4 weeks**. Uncollected Custom Pak orders are considered abandoned; no refund; active subscriptions associated with the order are deactivated to prevent further charges.
- Notification: OHS reminds the customer, but pickup responsibility is the customer's.

### 9. Shipping insurance
- **Highly recommended.** With insurance, the package is fully covered for loss or damage.
- Claims process: customer contacts the carrier first, then OHS files the claim and dispatches a replacement.
- **Without insurance, no replacement** for damage, loss, etc.
- Some protection may still be available without insurance — customer contacts carrier first, then OHS support for next steps.
- **Packages delivered to the correct address per carrier tracking are not covered** (includes stolen packages once delivery is confirmed).

## Pitfalls
- **Do not promise a delivery date.** Estimates are not guarantees. Direct the customer to the carrier with the tracking number.
- **Do not promise a refund for an incorrect-address shipment** unless OHS is at fault.
- **Do not promise a refund for a Custom Pak** once shipped — Custom Paks are made-to-order and not eligible for refund (see `ohs-customer-support/return-policy`).
- **Do not promise international tax/duty coverage.** Customer is responsible.
- **For lost or damaged packages: insurance first.** If no insurance was purchased, the customer should still contact the carrier, then support@optimalhealthsystems.com, but replacement is not guaranteed.

## Anything else

### Quick answers to common shipping questions
- **"When will my order ship?"** Within 48 hours of order (72 hours for custom orders).
- **"What are my shipping charges?"** $7.95 standard. Expedited options vary by destination / weight.
- **"Can I pay with [X]?"** OHS ships once payment is received; payment options are presented at checkout.
- **"What's your return policy?"** See `ohs-customer-support/return-policy`.
- **"Do you ship to [country]?"** Yes, international shipping is available, but customer pays all taxes / duties.
