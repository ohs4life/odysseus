# OHS Return & Refund Policy

## When to Use
When the user (employee or customer) asks any variant of:
- "What's your return policy?"
- "Can I get a refund?"
- "I want to return [product]."
- "My item arrived damaged / missing / wrong."
- "How long do I have to return?"
- "When will I see my refund?"

## Procedure

### 1. The headline rules (in priority order)

| Product type | Window | Refund |
|---|---|---|
| **Customized products** (e.g., Custom Health Paks) | All sales **final** after order placed | **No refund, no cancellation** (custom nature) |
| **Test Kits** (Optimal DNA, PlasticTest, etc.) | All sales **final** once shipped | **No return, no refund** |
| **Opened bottled product** (sold direct by OHS, at least half remaining) | Within **30 days** of purchase | **Full refund** of product cost (not handling/shipping) |
| **Unopened bottled product** (sold direct by OHS) | Within **90 days** of purchase | **Full refund** |
| **After 90 days** | — | **No refunds of any kind** (opened or unopened, partial or full) |
| **Sale items** (if return is granted) | — | Refunded at the **discounted cost paid** |

**Empty bottles / containers / bags are not eligible for refund.** If OHS receives empty packaging, the return is rejected.

### 2. Damages, defects, wrong items
- Items that are **defective when shipped** or items the customer **did not order but received** may qualify for an **immediate refund**.
- For missing, damaged, or wrong shipments: customer must contact OHS **immediately** by email at `support@optimalhealthsystems.com` or phone at `1-800-890-4547`.

### 3. Refund processing timeline
- Once the return is received, the OHS quality-control team assesses it. If the return qualifies, the refund is issued to the **original payment method within 10 business days**.
- OHS business hours: **Mon–Thu 8am–5pm, Fri 8am–4:30pm Arizona time**.
- After refund is initiated, the customer's bank may take an additional **3–5 business days** to post the credit.
- If **more than 15 business days** have passed since OHS approved the return, customer should contact `support@optimalhealthsystems.com`.
- OHS **does not** send a refund-confirmation email unless the customer requests one.

### 4. Products bought through a health professional
- If purchased through a health professional, customer should reach out to **them** for guidance on returning the product or getting a refund. OHS handles this through the referring professional, not directly.

### 5. How to start a return
1. Contact OHS by email (`support@optimalhealthsystems.com`) or phone (`1-800-890-4547`).
2. Ship the return to the OHS return address (see below).
3. **Include the customer's name and email inside the package** so OHS can look up the account.
4. **Include the packing slip or order number** if available — speeds processing.
5. **Customer pays return shipping** unless OHS is at fault or an exception is made by the Customer Success Manager.
6. **Items must be returned within 30 days of contacting OHS** about the refund.

### 6. End-of-window exception
If the customer has an opened product close to the end of the 30-day window and is worried the return won't arrive in time, customer should contact OHS so a note can be made that the product is on its way. OHS will honor the return as long as other conditions are met.

### 7. In-store pickups
- **No Custom Pak:** customer has **3 days** to pick up. After 3 days, order is canceled and refunded to original payment.
- **With Custom Pak:** customer has **4 weeks** to pick up after notification. Uncollected Custom Pak orders are considered abandoned, **no refund**, and any active subscriptions are deactivated before the next renewal to prevent further charges.

## Pitfalls
- **Never promise a refund on a Custom Pak or Test Kit.** These are all-sales-final per the policy.
- **Never promise to refund handling / shipping fees.** Product cost only.
- **Never promise the refund will be visible in less than 3–5 business days** after OHS initiates it (bank posting time is outside OHS's control).
- **Never promise a refund for a product purchased through a health professional** without looping in that professional.
- **Never promise return-shipping coverage** unless OHS is at fault or the Customer Success Manager has made an exception.
- **Empty bottles are not refundable** — confirm the customer has at least half the product remaining.

## Anything else

### Common return questions and answers

- **"I just got my order and I changed my mind — can I return?"** If unopened, yes, within 90 days. If opened with at least half remaining, within 30 days. If it's a Custom Pak or Test Kit, no.
- **"I opened it and used a few capsules — can I return the rest?"** Yes, if at least half the product remains and within 30 days.
- **"It's been 4 months — can I return?"** No. After 90 days, no refunds of any kind.
- **"The bottle arrived empty / damaged."** Contact OHS immediately at `support@optimalhealthsystems.com` — this is a defect-on-shipment case and may qualify for immediate refund.
- **"I bought this from my chiropractor — can I return it directly to OHS?"** No — go back to the chiropractor. OHS handles returns through the referring professional.
