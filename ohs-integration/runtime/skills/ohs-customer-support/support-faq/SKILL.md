# OHS Customer Support — General FAQ

## When to Use
When the user (employee or customer) asks any variant of:
- "Do I have to pay sales tax?"
- "Where can I find my receipt / invoice?"
- "When will my credit card be charged?"
- "When will my return credit appear?"
- "How do I find a product on the site?"
- "How do I use a coupon?"
- "What's D.O.M.? / Where is the expiration date on the bottle?"
- "Do you have a low-price guarantee?"
- "What's your privacy policy?"
- "Is the website secure?"

## Procedure

### 1. Pricing & billing
- **Sales tax:** only charged to customers in **Arizona** (the same state as the OHS warehouse).
- **Credit card charge timing:** card is charged **within 24 hours prior to shipment** (not at order placement).
- **Return credit timing:** credits usually take **7–10 business days** from the time OHS receives the item(s).
- **Receipt / invoice:** under the customer's account (top-right icon), click **Order Status** to view orders and print invoices.
- **Charge questions:** under the account, compare order history on the OHS website with the customer's financial records. If questions remain, contact customer service.

### 2. Site navigation
- **Find a product:** use the navigation menus (top + bottom of the site) or the search box. If trouble, contact customer service.
- **Clickable links:** mouse cursor becomes a "hand" over a link, "arrow" otherwise.
- **Coupons:** add items to cart, click the cart icon (top-right), enter the coupon code in the box at the bottom of the cart.

### 3. Product dating — D.O.M. and expiration
- **D.O.M. (Date of Manufacturing):** printed on every bottle per FDA requirements, at the **far end of the label**.
- **Expiration date:** **2 years from the D.O.M.**
- **After 2 years:** the product is **not harmful** to take, but it may start to **decrease in potency**.

### 4. Low Price Guarantee
- "It is our commitment to provide you with the best value, at the best price. We value your business and strive to keep our prices low, and SAVE you money!"
- This is a positioning statement, not a formal price-match program. Don't promise a specific match; route specific price-match questions to `support@optimalhealthsystems.com`.

### 5. Privacy, security, and lab-data handling
- **Website security:** protected with **SSL (secure socket layer) encryption** — the highest standard in internet security (as of the original OHS copy).
- **General privacy:** see the OHS Privacy Policy (link on the website). Do not paraphrase the policy; route to the link or to `support@optimalhealthsystems.com`.
- **Lab / DNA data handling (per OHS):**
  - OHS takes handling of private information "very seriously."
  - The lab is concerned about customer privacy: OHS "takes great lengths to destroy and dispose of your DNA, blood, and/or other samples as soon as your report is complete."
  - OHS does not provide or sell data to third-party companies for research.
  - Customer data is stored in OHS's "HIPAA-compliant Health Portal." **Only the customer and their referring health professional (if applicable) have access.**

  > ⚠️ **Note for the agent:** OHS uses the term "HIPAA-compliant" loosely. OHS is **not** a HIPAA covered entity (see `docs/OHSS-CONTEXT.md` and `ohs-compliance/disclaimers`). The right framing is "privacy-protected" + the GINA / state-privacy rules, not "HIPAA-compliant." Use this skill's privacy language as a starting point, but apply the GINA framing from `ohs-compliance/disclaimers` whenever DNA comes up.

## Pitfalls
- **Do not promise a specific date for a return credit to appear** — 7–10 business days is the OHS-published window, but bank processing adds time.
- **Do not claim "HIPAA-compliant" verbatim to a customer** without the disclaimer that OHS uses the term in a privacy sense, not as a regulatory status. The right framing is "your data is protected" + GINA / state-privacy.
- **Do not promise a specific D.O.M. format** — different products may have different label formats; the rule is "far end of the label."
- **Do not promise a low-price match** — the guarantee is a positioning statement, not a formal program.
- **Do not state a specific SSL/TLS version** unless verified — just say "encrypted."

## Anything else

### Quick answers

- **"Do I have to pay sales tax?"** Only if you're in Arizona.
- **"Where is my receipt?"** Account → Order Status → print invoice.
- **"When is my card charged?"** Within 24 hours of shipment.
- **"When does my return credit show up?"** 7–10 business days from when OHS receives the return.
- **"How do I use a coupon?"** Add items to cart → cart icon (top right) → enter code at bottom of cart.
- **"Where is the expiration date?"** It's not printed — D.O.M. is on the label (far end), and expiration is 2 years from D.O.M.
- **"Is the site secure?"** Yes, SSL encryption.
- **"What's your privacy policy?"** See the OHS Privacy Policy (linked from the website). For DNA / lab data specifically, see `ohs-compliance/disclaimers` (GINA section).
